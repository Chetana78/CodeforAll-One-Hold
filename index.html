OneHold

Most med apps need two hands, tiny taps, and swipes. OneHold is built for limited grip, tremor, and one-thumb use.

  • Hold to confirm — not a 16px checkbox
  • No drag, pinch, or swipe-required actions
  • Voice and keyboard/switch as backups
  • Undo instead of punishing mis-taps
